Within a line, keep every written word on the paper and frame the whole phrase so far; hold each hero whole and inside title-safe from its `end` until the next phrase starts. No frame without a complete word.
2. **The lyric is typeset text under the traces, not the trace becoming letters** (40.80: the cardio line runs on above BUTT…). Fix: the cardio trace leaves its baseline and climbs into the letters in one continuous line, dropping back into its channel after each word.

Fix: grid in silver at ~30%, channel ink 2–3 px film-black; at least four setups eased into downbeats: macro on the cardio nib, wide on four channels with the pen arms, a low raking angle along the paper for the butterflies shiver (now it reads as a warped image), the under view.

## Plate 6 `uv`: REWORK of light and framing (keep the timing script and beat logic, which work)

1. **A saturated violet flood over the whole cloth** (every frame; 66.20 clearest): the neon risk in rule 6. Fix: the cloth near film black outside a hand-held beam pool ~40% of frame width, soft-edged, moving with each sweep; inside the pool, dark low-saturation uv; fluor glows only in the pool and fades ~0.5 s behind it.
2. **The lamp reads as a ceiling tube along the top edge; the glove is a black silhouette** (66.20, 71.45). Fix: a short UV tube in a black housing with a handle, held in the matte nitrile glove, rim-lit by its own tube, in frame on every downbeat framing.
3. **The swab works a separate zebra-striped strip under HANDS** (71.00–72.20); a dark spike under the word at 71.45 reads as debris. Fix: each stroke crosses the hero's own trace; the swabbed band of the letters goes dark and the cotton tip takes the glow. Delete the separate strip.
4. **Traces read as printed sign type** (blurred, thresholded Archivo Black gives bubbly letters). Fix: keep Archivo but make the strokes uneven, weave showing through, brightness 60–100%, soft broken edges. No droplets or spatter.
5. **One composition across 8 bars** (a centred word on a top-down cloth). Fix: 4–5 setups, e.g. a table-height raking shot with the beam sweeping toward the lens, a three-quarter view of the bench with lamp and glove, a macro on the weave as the beam edge uncovers letters, a swab close-up, then GO. Heroes off-centre, varied in scale. Avoid the top-down view (plate 3 is fabric from above).
6. **At 64.10 DIRTY lingers under "but your"** (reads "but your DIRTY"). Fix: DIRTY sinks to dark as the beam leaves, before "but" at 63.83.
7. **The cloth does not read as a pillowcase** (faceted, low-poly folds). Fix: show the hem, the open end and the side seam; soften the folds; the one label EXHIBIT 5 on a tag.
