## Frame checker (`render.ts lint`, `app/scripts/lint.ts`, 2026-10-08)

`cd app && bun scripts/render.ts lint [--only ids] [--from A --to B] [--fps 30] [--out ../out/lint/x.json] [--strict]` (in the cloud add `CHROME_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` and `--swiftshader`). It steps the scenes frame by frame with the draw calls stubbed out (scene logic only: the whole video took 16 s in the cloud container, boot included), finds every stage in every scene (objects with `render`, `words`, `cam`), and after each stage render reads every shown 3D letter: its extent on screen, its front face as a screen polygon, how squarely it faces the camera (both clipped at a clip plane in the letter's shader, as in circe's wine). Words drawn with `fillText` during a scene's render (`drawPhrase`) are recorded for the timing checks. Each run word is matched to the sung word (contiguous match nearest to when it first shows). It prints one line per finding (ERROR / WARN / INFO, time span, word with line.word index, detail) and writes the JSON (findings, cuts, a sung-vs-shown table of every word) to `--out`; `--strict` exits 1 on errors.
- Errors: EARLY (on screen > 0.4 s before it is sung; checklist 1), PRE-CUT POP (new on screen 1–2 frames before a cut; 7), COLLISION (front faces of two words' letters overlapping > 20% of the smaller face and > 150 px² for 3+ frames; 5), LEFTOVER (one letter left for 0.1 s+ after the rest of its word went; 6).
- Warnings: EARLY (> 0.11 s), LATE (> 0.1 s after the onset), MISSING (sung inside a loaded scene, never on screen), TWO LINES (two lyric lines not sung together on screen > 0.3 s; 2), short COLLISION, PARTIAL (a word missing letters > 0.2 s, build-ups included), BLINK (fully on screen < 0.2 s), TINY (< 14 px), EDGE-ON (face > 75 degrees off square for 0.1 s; 3, 6), BACKWARDS (seen from behind), CROP (cut by the frame edge), TITLE-SAFE (inside 96 px of the edge). INFO: shown words that match no sung word.
- Cuts: a change of scene or stage, or a one-frame camera jump (turn > 12 degrees or a move > 15% of the distance to the letters, at least 2.5 times the camera's motion on the frames either side; a fast crane is not a cut). The cuts found are printed: check that list against the plan.

## Gotchas learned

- `render.ts`'s private server (started when no `--url` answers): until d6ef089 it was spawned as `bunx vite`, and on Windows `proc.kill()` killed only the `bunx` wrapper, leaving `bun "…\vite\bin\vite.js" --port 5xxx --strictPort` running after every render. 40 piled up over two sessions (2.7 GB) and renders failed for lack of memory. vite now runs directly in bun (`process.execPath`). Check for leftovers in PowerShell: `Get-CimInstance Win32_Process -Filter "Name = 'bun.exe'" | ? { $_.CommandLine -like '*vite.js*--strictPort*' }`. Stopping processes needs the client's OK (the permission check blocks agents from it), and never by name (their Chrome may be open).
- GLSL_COMMON already defines `heat()`: don't name a uniform `heat`.
- `hatch(u, 0)` still draws a faint hairline (AA at zero width): multiply by `smoothstep(0.02, 0.08, darkness)` where unlit must be black.
- A missing scene renders dark red (placeholder), not a bug.
- `cut()` in the timeline floors to the beat at/before a line's first word; pickups move cuts up to a beat early.
- Layer2D channel tricks used: green = "casts a shadow" (hook v1), red−green = incised line inside black slip (hook n=3).
- Black-figure letters (hook's reflection): `Word3D` option `incise` builds `incTex` (each glyph in black with a red contour ring inside it, from the same atlas layout); the letter shader's `bf` uniform (1) draws black slip with that contour scratched back to the clay, unlit. `incTex` is assigned to the letters after their uniforms are cloned: `UniformsUtils.clone` would copy the texture once per letter.
- `WATER_HOOK`: a scene that defines it in its hooks GLSL supplies `waterHook(px, ruv, rt, col)`, called on the water after the reflection is mixed in; `ruv` is the swell-bent reflection uv (where the swell moved what this point mirrors), `rt` the mirrored render there. Paint anything that should sit in the reflection with `ruv`, not `FRAG_PX`, or it will not ripple with the rest.
- `Stage.render(..., { mirror: { before, after } })` runs around the mirrored scene render, so a reflection can differ from its word: hook hides the "?" mesh and sets `bf` (n=1) and poses the word 0.15 s ahead (n=2), then restores both in `after`.
- `SHORE` (exported from `stage.ts`: the far shore's z and the camera's y, z, pitch) is the single source of truth for the sea→hook hand-off frame; `sea.ts` ends on it and `hook.ts` starts from it. Change it there only.
- The artifact host refuses XML files with a DOCTYPE (bench-pack strips it from the stroke-font SVG copies).
- faster-whisper's own decoder breaks with newer PyAV: pass an array (done in `whisper_run.py`).
- A wait loop like `until ! pgrep -f "render.ts video"` matches its own command line and never exits: use `pgrep -f "[r]ender.ts video"`.
- The no-HMR render server (`PDOOM_NO_HMR=1 bunx vite --port 5190`) does not see newly created scene files: restart it after adding one. It must stay up for a whole `video` render (the page keeps fetching from it), and a background command is killed after 2 h: restart the server before any long render if it has been up for more than ~1.5 h.
- `Stage` draws letter meshes after the floor/wall pass: a flame that is behind letters must be drawn first (`flameBehind: true`), or it shows through them.
- Floor engraving at grazing angles aliases; `pxLines` fades lines closer than ~3 px into their mean tone, and hooks should widen their AA by `gPix`.
- (Cloud) The container can restart mid-turn and kills every background job (server and renders): render long clips in segments (`app/scripts/review-v7.sh` pattern) and restart the server first.
- GLSL `pow(x, y)` is undefined for x < 0 (SwiftShader returned |x|^y): souls' shades appeared giant while rising. Write cubes and squares as products.
- A degenerate bevel triangle in an extruded letter has a zero normal; `normalize` gives NaN, which blooms into a white star. It showed only in the mirrored scene render (the glaze reflection sees the letters' undersides). The letter and token shaders now guard normals and NaN/Inf colours; keep that in any new mesh shader.
- The unseen key light's highlight on black glaze is its mirror image, a flame-like blob: keep `spec` ≈ 0.05 in key-lit shots; glazed letter faces use a narrow dim sheen for the same reason.
- Never put `pkill -f <pattern>` in the same Bash command as the thing it should not kill: the pattern matches the command's own shell (`pkill -f "[v]ite --port 5190"; ... bunx vite --port 5190` killed itself, exit 144). Kill in one call, start the server in another.
- Typecheck before committing: `cd app && bunx tsc --noEmit -p . && bunx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.scripts.json` (both clean at handoff).
- Windows: ffprobe ends its lines with CRLF; strip `\r` before string compares or `$((...))` in bash (`review-v7.sh` does). Git Bash has no `pgrep`/`pkill`: kill one specific PID with `taskkill //PID <pid> //F`, never by name (the client's own Chrome is open).
- An agent writing about the horse plate was stopped once by the API's content filter ("Output blocked by content filtering policy"), most likely by the lyric's violent wording in its own prose; resuming it with "keep your prose neutral: refer to lines by their hero words and times" worked. Put that line in prompts for plates with violent lyrics (horse, wall).
- Torch on a busy CPU: `ctc_emissions.py` ran 20 minutes without finishing one model while renders used the cores (OpenMP threads spin-wait when oversubscribed). Run it with `OMP_WAIT_POLICY=PASSIVE` and fewer threads, or when nothing else runs; then each model takes ~1–3 min.
- In a git worktree, let `render.ts` start its own private server (don't pass `--url` to a server started from another checkout: it serves that checkout's files). Under heavy load SwiftShader stills can time out (Playwright's 30 s screenshot timeout): rerun them.
- `fwidth` inside divergent branches returns garbage on SwiftShader: compute AA widths (`gPix`) before branching (it hid `circe`'s rim decoration at first).
- `render.ts lint` stubs `renderer.render`/`clear`: a scene whose logic read pixels back would break under it (none does). It still needs WebGL to boot the scenes (`--swiftshader` in the cloud).
- On a low camera over the water a nearly flat letter shows its lit top as a block: hide letters with `hinge > 1.2` in every floating phrase, rising or folding (`hideFlat` in `hook.ts`). For exits under ~0.1 s, `popWords`' default 10 ms ripple per letter is a large share of the fold and makes a phrase vanish piecemeal: pass `exitRipple: 0`.
